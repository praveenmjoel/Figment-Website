Curriculum
Two sections.
Eight domains.

The Digital SAT replaced the paper SAT in 2024. Shorter, adaptive, and Bluebook-based — it requires a very different preparation strategy. Our curriculum is built specifically for this format.

01
Reading & Writing
54 Questions · Approx. 64 Minutes · Two Adaptive Modules
Evidence-based reading, grammar and usage, and vocabulary-in-context. The adaptive engine means your second module adjusts to your first — we train you to perform consistently across all difficulty levels.
  • Craft & Structure ~28%
    Words in context · Text structure & purpose · Cross-text connections
  • Information & Ideas ~26%
    Central ideas & details · Command of evidence (textual & quantitative) · Inferences
  • Standard English Conventions ~26%
    Sentence boundaries & punctuation · Subject-verb & pronoun agreement · Verb tense & modifier placement
  • Expression of Ideas ~20%
    Transitions & logical flow · Rhetorical synthesis · Effective & precise language
02
Mathematics
44 Questions · Approx. 70 Minutes · Calculator Permitted Throughout
Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem Solving, Geometry, and Trigonometry. The Digital SAT allows a calculator throughout — but speed and accuracy still matter. We build systematic problem-solving habits, not formula recall.
  • Algebra ~35%
    Linear equations & inequalities · Systems of equations · Linear functions & graphs
  • Advanced Math ~35%
    Quadratic & polynomial equations · Exponential functions · Equivalent expressions & operations
  • Problem-Solving & Data Analysis ~15%
    Ratios, rates & proportions · Statistics & probability · Data tables & graphs
  • Geometry & Trigonometry ~15%
    Area, volume & surface area · Lines, angles & triangles · Sine, cosine & basic trig ratios

Score range: 400 – 1600  ·  Reading & Writing: 200–800  ·  Math: 200–800  ·  Duration: 2 hrs 14 min  ·  Format: Adaptive (Bluebook app)
